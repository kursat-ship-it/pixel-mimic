# AGENTS.md: onboarding for agents working on @hayret.piksel

You are joining a project that makes **coded pixel-art science Reels** for Kürşat's Turkish channel **@hayret.piksel**.
Everything (picture, chiptune music, SFX, narration text) is generated by the `bitloop` engine from one spec on one beat
clock. This file is the contract: the workflow, who does what, every standing rule from Kürşat, the QC checklist and the
lessons learned from the first four reels. Read it fully before touching anything.

Related docs: [`docs/WORKFLOW.md`](docs/WORKFLOW.md) (checklist per topic) · [`docs/ENGINE.md`](docs/ENGINE.md) (modules +
spec fields) · [`recipe/recipe.md`](recipe/recipe.md) **§12 = the authoring guide** (Turkish) ·
[`recipe/RESEARCHER_GUIDE.md`](recipe/RESEARCHER_GUIDE.md) · [`brand/BRAND.md`](brand/BRAND.md) · [`bitloop/README.md`](bitloop/README.md) (full engine reference).

## 0. Ground rules (never break these)
1. **Never post, publish, upload, send or share anything without Kürşat's explicit approval.** Deliver files and a summary;
   he decides. `out/upload.json` always has `"posted": false` (written by `tools/finalize_package.py`).
2. **Talk to Kürşat in Turkish** (tr-TR). All **on-screen text is Turkish**. Code, comments and agent docs may be English.
3. **Facts must be sourced** (`sources.md`). No overclaims; contested claims are softened ("savundu (tartışmalı)",
   "yapabilir", "algılar" instead of "görür"). Mark paraphrase vs verbatim quote.
4. **Edit `build_spec.py`, never `spec.json` by hand** (it is regenerated). Keep `spec_proxy.json` in sync (the builder writes both).
5. **Engine extension rule:** every new engine parameter must **default to the old behaviour** (old specs/reels render
   byte-for-byte the same) and must get a **test** in `bitloop/tests/`. All tests must pass before any render is delivered.
6. Keep the previous delivery when re-rendering (`out/<slug>_v1.mp4`); never overwrite the only good copy.

## 1. Kürşat's standing rules (accumulated; all still active)
### Format
- **30 fps** (not 60). 1080×1920, art canvas 180×320 logical px, ×6 nearest-neighbour (`format.grid = 6`). Pixels stay square:
  the camera redraws geometry, it never stretches bitmaps.
- Length: sweet spot 20–45 s (validator warns outside, errors outside 15–90 s). The calm reels ran 48–49 s; Kürşat was told
  and offered cuts. Say so if you go over.
### Look and brand (fixed layer)
- **Rich, multi-layered scenes**: every scene ≥ 3 moving layers (back parallax / subject / front particles + ambient). Validator errors below 3.
- **Brand palette "Çini Gecesi"** (17 colours, full table in `brand/BRAND.md`). Key colours:
  Gece `#060814` · Kobalt `#2C47A6` · Krem `#F4ECDC` (text) · Türkuaz Işık `#41E8D2` (energy / key number / `*highlight*`) ·
  Çini Mercanı `#EC4A57` · Mercan Işık `#FF9A86` (`_second highlight_`) · Kutu `#04060E` (text box).
  ~70 % of the frame in cobalt-night tones; türkuaz grows over the film; mercan is rare and warm.
- **Per-topic palettes are allowed** (Kürşat: "Farklı renk paletleri deniyelim"): `spec.palette` or `segments[].palette`
  (name or dict). The brand layer stays constant: narration box/text colours, logo, sonic logo, tag. Every custom palette
  must contain `#04060E` and `#F4ECDC`. Make a `palette_board.png` (engine stills per palette) for review.
- **Logo "hayret gözü"**: almond eye, krem outline, türkuaz iris, **mercan "!" as the pupil**, 3 sparks.
- **The `@hayret.piksel` tag is diegetic, never a watermark**: it lives on an object in the world (product label, neon sign,
  chalkboard corner, lab plate, ship hull, hive label, terminal boot line…). Be creative and make it fit the scene logic.
  At least **1 legible** placement (on phones: ≥ 2 dots ≈ 12 px tall, ≥ 7:1 contrast), at most 3 total (1 legible + 1–2 subtle),
  none in the first 2 s, **never in captions or narration**. Validator enforces most of this.
### Text (Oct 21 feedback: biggest change so far)
- Kürşat: forcing short captions made things unclear. **Use fuller, narrator-style sentences** (`segments[].narration`), not
  telegraph captions. Text appears **letter by letter or word by word on the music grid** (`reveal: type|word|line`,
  `rate: 16th|8th|beat…`) with soft blips, like a narrator. Put it in **fitting regions** (top box, RPG dialog with a speaker,
  bottom panel, title card, callout pointing at an object, or in-world on a board/screen/book).
- **Turkish only, built-in 5×7 pixel font** (`bitloop/bitloop/pixfont.py`, full-height İ Ş Ğ Ü Ö Ç Â Î Û; `minifont.py` 3×5 for
  tiny in-world labels). **Do not use Press Start 2P (or other TTFs) for on-screen text: it breaks Ş Ğ Ü Ö Ç.** (A TTF is only
  used for ASCII labels on review boards.) Use `case: "upper"` (Turkish-aware i→İ, ı→I) only for title cards.
- Sizes: body 6 px/dot (~24 chars × ≤ 5 lines), head 9 (~15 × ≤ 3), title 12. **Legibility floor 5 px/dot** (`narration.MIN_DOT`).
- **Reading time:** after the block is fully revealed it stays ≥ **0.6 s + 0.35 s per word** (validator warns).
- **Safe zones:** nothing important in the **bottom 18 %** (platform caption/buttons), right 12.5 % (action column),
  top/left 5 %. That includes subjects (characters' feet!), not only text.
- Hook text: first letter ≤ 0.2 s, fully readable by ~3 s, ≤ ~12 words, `head` size.
### Motion and pacing (Oct 21 feedback: "Hareketi biraz azalt, çok hızlı olmuş")
- `"pacing": "calm"` is the default (soft shake/flash, slower parallax, longer transitions). New shot every ~3–5 s; average
  visual change ~3 s (a revealing sentence counts as change); fast transitions (crash whip spin glitch flash mosaic portal split)
  at most ~1 per 10 s, saved for hook / twist / reveal / drop. Small, slow camera moves (e.g. zoom `[1, 1.15]` over 4 s).
  `"punchy"` exists only for the first two reels.
- Still: **many beat-synced transitions** (17 types, each with its own SFX), ≥ 3 types per film, never the same one 3× in a row.
### Music and sound
- **Every video gets completely new music designed for its topic**: tempo/tempo map, meter (aksak allowed), key, scale or
  makam (23 scales incl. hicaz, uşşak, kürdi, nihavend, rast, hüzzam, saba), chip flavour (2a03 / gameboy / sid / fm), voices,
  drums, sections. Do not reuse the previous reel's settings; let the music *explain* the idea (e.g. a melody written by a series).
- **Sonic logo (fixed):** 4 notes, scale degrees **5→1→2→3**, rhythm **short-short-short-LONG** (½ ½ ½ + 1¾ beats),
  transposed into the current section's key/scale and played on the video's lead timbre, at the **reveal** and at the **loop seam**
  (`music.logo.at = ["reveal","loop"]`).
- **Mastering:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP after AAC (engine: two-pass loudnorm + AAC-safe limiter, `limit_ceiling`).
### Edit craft (act like a professional short-form creator)
- **Strong, creative hook** in 0.5–1.5 s: motion + a subject on frame 1 (no `void`, no logo intro), a pattern-breaker, a
  curiosity gap (question / bold claim / number).
- **Re-hooks every 5–8 s** (new question, number, scale, reveal, drop), an **open loop** promised early and paid late, a
  **mid-video twist** (30–80 %), a **payoff** in the second half with the sonic logo + legible tag, rising stakes.
- **Seamless loop:** last frame flows into frame 0 (`loop_transition`, `rewind_to_frame0`, `layer_sync: "first"`), hook text returns.
- Metadata: title ≤ 60 chars, curiosity + a real number, 🎧-type emoji; 2-sentence description with the fact + nuance +
  a comment prompt 👇; 3–6 hashtags incl. `#hayretpiksel`; `meta.cover_t` = strongest frame (subject + ≤ 3 words).

## 2. Workflow and role split (per topic)
Kürşat gives a topic → the orchestrator splits the work into **separate agents**. This split worked best:

| Role | Does | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Researcher / editor** (planner) | Researches the science with sources; finds the honest answer and the "hayret" moment; designs music + edit on one beat clock; writes all Turkish narration with reading-time math; picks scenes, transitions, palette, tag placement | `plan_tr.md`, `sources.md`, `build_spec.py` → `spec.json` + `spec_proxy.json`, `retention_map.txt` (validator output), `storyboard.png`, `render_brief.md` (what new engine work is needed), optional `palette_board.png` |
| **Orchestrator** | Shows Kürşat the scene plan + retention map + title options (in Turkish) **and waits for approval** before the render | approved plan |
| **Render coder** | Implements new scenes/params in `bitloop/` (defaults = old behaviour, + tests), renders | engine code + tests, `out/<slug>_master.mp4`, `out/<slug>.mp4`, `out/cover.png`, `out/contact_sheet.png`, `out/upload.json`, list of known issues |
| **Independent QC** (fresh agent, not the coder) | Measures and watches everything, **fixes problems itself and re-renders**, keeps the previous upload as `out/<slug>_v1.mp4`, scores v1 vs v2 | `qc_report.md`, v2 files |
| **Orchestrator** | Delivers the video file + contact sheet + short Turkish summary (fixes, remaining issues, open choices with a default). States clearly that **nothing was posted** | — |

Hand-offs are files in `projects/<slug>/`. Each agent reads the previous agent's files, not chat history. Details per step:
[`docs/WORKFLOW.md`](docs/WORKFLOW.md).

## 3. Delivery specs
- **Master:** `bitloop render` default, libx264 CRF 18, preset medium, `-tune animation`, High@4.2, yuv420p, bt709 tags, AAC 192k 48 kHz, `+faststart`.
- **Upload encode (what Kürşat posts):** from a near-lossless intermediate (`render --crf 4`), **two-pass H.264 High 4.2 at
  ~4.4 Mb/s** (`-preset slow -b:v 4400k -maxrate 9000k -bufsize 9000k -tune animation -x264-params no-deblock=1:psy-rd=0.6,0:aq-mode=2 -g 60`),
  yuv420p, bt709 primaries/trc/matrix, audio copied (AAC 192k), `+faststart`. Result ≈ 23–28 MB for 40–49 s,
  SSIM ≥ ~0.95 vs the intermediate. Script: `tools/encode_upload.sh`; whole chain: `tools/make_master.sh projects/<slug>`.
- Deliver: `out/<slug>.mp4` (upload), `out/<slug>_master.mp4`, `out/cover.png` (1080×1920 at `meta.cover_t`),
  `out/contact_sheet.png`, `out/upload.json` (`posted: false`). Videos are **not** committed to git.

## 4. QC checklist and scoring
QC scores each category 1–10 for v1 and v2 with a one-line reason (see any `projects/*/qc_report.md`):
**Görsel (visual) · Retention · Ses (audio) · Bilgi doğruluğu / kaynak (facts/sources) · Teslim (delivery) · Metadata.**
Report also: "was Kürşat's feedback applied?" (narration, reading time, placements, calm pacing, palette), fixes with
reasons, remaining issues, verification numbers, files, and the verdict ("Yayına hazır (onay sende). Paylaşım yapılmadı.").

Measure (tools in `tools/qc/`, see `docs/WORKFLOW.md` §5):
- **Integrity:** frame count = duration × 30, video duration == audio duration, 0 decode errors, bt709 tags, faststart (`integrity.sh`).
  A render that was interrupted must be re-checked end to end.
- **Visual:** contact sheet + strips around every cut/hit (`bitloop sheet/strip`); no near-black frames (lum < 12/255) unless
  intentional (e.g. CRT off); longest near-static run < 2 s outside planned holds; loop-seam diff ≈ median frame diff;
  per-segment motion (`video_stats.py --spec`). Look at **phone size**: every text, tag and label legible; nothing in the bottom 18 %.
- **Retention:** `bitloop validate` (0 errors), hook ≤ 1.5 s, re-hook gaps ≤ 8 s, twist, payoff, fast-transition budget, reading times.
- **Audio:** −14 ±0.5 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP, LRA, per-segment loudness (does the drop land? is the quiet moment quieter?),
  **phone-speaker simulation** (300 Hz high-pass; loss > ~5 LU = too bass-heavy) (`loudness.py --spec`), stems for any
  voice that must be heard (`stems.py`), clicks, HF harshness.
- **A/V sync:** cross-correlation lag must be 0 frames (`av_sync.py`). Whole-film r is weak (≈ 0.05) in calm reels because the
  picture moves continuously; then check cuts/hits by eye in strips. Musical events that the picture shows (lit notes,
  sonar rings, ball split) must land on the same frame as the sound.
- **Facts:** every on-screen claim and description sentence against `sources.md`; numbers, years, quote vs paraphrase.
- **Delivery & metadata:** SSIM of upload vs intermediate (`ssim.sh`), size, cover is the intended frame, title/description/hashtags.

## 5. Lessons learned (real findings from the four QC reports)
Visual / text
- **Snap small text to the pixel grid.** Tiny labels under zoom dithered into mush (".piksel" read ".pikse]"). Fix: `snap=True`
  in `bitmap.draw_bitmap/draw_text/draw_mark`, `minifont.draw_mini` (each dot exactly k×k pixels). Legible tags are snapped.
- **Glow and vignette eat labels.** Clear the glow buffer under readable labels and exempt them from the vignette
  (`render._matte_glow` + `Canvas.keep`); a hive label was at 78 % brightness and checkered before this.
- **Use the 5×7 Turkish font for labels that must read.** The 3×5 micro font garbled "GÖRELİLİK" ("GÜRELILIK"); chalk formulas
  needed 2× size (`formula_dot`, `label_font`, `note_font` params in `chalk_unify`). Remove micro-notes that no one can read.
- **No black frames in transitions**: the portal transition left a black border (fixed: live camera dive); `flash` peak is capped
  at 0.88 so no frame is a flat cream card; `spin` has `over:true` (no black gap); CRT `floor` keeps an after-image.
- **Stay on palette.** Cream→Sis blends on dice went off-palette (blue dither); use flat palette tones on small objects.
- **Text must not cover the subject** (a mosaic box hid the flowers; a panel covers a sweater); set `subject`, `xy`, crop.
- **Bottom UI zone applies to characters too** (a hero at 0.84–0.98 H, a kid at 0.88–0.97 H were moved up).
- **Easing can linger**: `inOutCubic` on a 4.6× zoom sat on giant pixels ~0.5 s → `outCubic`.
- **Busy frames:** a "board of boards" and its cover were noisy → `far_ca` / `far_dots` to calm neighbours. Check the cover.
- **Pose/continuity jumps inside transitions** (kid pose switch inside an iris) → `rewind_turn`. Check every seam frame-by-frame.
- **Show, don't only tell**: when the plan says "the ball disappears", show the comparison (a 1.25-beat `peek`).
- **Thermal view needs AGC** (auto gain, percentiles 3–99.7) or the snake tile is a flat blob; adaptive bands in `mandel_zoom`
  stopped a late flat wall of colour.
- Fill empty lower thirds with something alive (e.g. pixel piano `keys`), but outside the UI zone.
Audio
- **A whisper/lookahead voice ~31 dB under the lead is inaudible.** Use a dedicated voice in the same register ~12 dB under the
  lead and an SFX in the 1.5–8 kHz band that survives phone speakers (`glitch`). Verify with stems.
- **Picture and music must diverge on the same beat** (twin balls split at beat 3, twin bells at beat 6 → re-seeded so both split together).
  Make twin voices timbrally different (FM ratio/index) and panned apart so the split is audible.
- **Flat mixes**: drop ≈ quiet scene in loudness. Use `sections[].level_db` (pre-SFX, ±40 ms crossfade) to shape an arc.
- HF harshness on chip edges: master high-shelf −3 dB at 7 kHz (`music.hf_shelf_db`). AAC true-peak overshoot is real; the
  limiter ceiling is set for AAC, so LRA stays low (~2–5 LU), acceptable for chiptune.
- Drop needs its breath (`music.breath`, held stop) to read; an SFX filling the breath weakens it.
Delivery
- **`--package` cover bug:** `cover_t` lives in `meta.cover_t`; old code took t = 1.0 s. Fixed in `cli.cover_t` (tested). Still
  open the cover and look at it.
- **bt709 tags** were missing on a master once; the encoder now writes them (`h264_metadata` bsf). `integrity.sh` checks.
- Keep `_v1` files; QC backups go outside the repo.
Facts
- Soften contested philosophy (Scriven "showed" → "argued (contested)"); give survey context (PhilPapers 2020 "accept or lean
  toward"); popular misquotes are not used (Einstein 1936 "comprehensibility" is translated from the original); false colour
  is called a translation; "görür" → "algılar" where the sense is not vision.

## 6. Engine in one paragraph
`spec.json` → `spec.load/normalise` (tempo map, sections, segments in beats → seconds, pacing preset, brand preset) →
`spec.validate` + `retention_map` → `music.build_audio` (synth voices + drums + series + sonic logo + SFX from
`spec.timeline_events()`, two-pass loudnorm) and `render.Film.frame(t)` (layers, scene, props, camera, transitions, vision,
narration, quantise to palette, ×6 upscale) → ffmpeg. Frames are pure functions of t → parallel workers, deterministic
(seeded). Module map and spec fields: [`docs/ENGINE.md`](docs/ENGINE.md).
